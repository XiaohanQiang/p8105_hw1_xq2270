penguins dataset includes three penguin species:Adelie, Chinstrap and
Gentoo. from three different island includes Biscoe, Dream and
Torgersen. Important variables include bill length (mm), bill depth(mm),
flipper length(mm), body mass(g), sex, and year. The mean flipper length
is 200.92 mm.

The mean of the numeric variable can be directly calculate. The mean of
the logic variable means the proportion of TRUE value. That’s because R
treats TRUE as 1 and FALSE as 0 while calculate the mean value. The
character and factor variables return NA with warnings because their
means are not defined.

``` r
as.numeric(hw2_df$positive)
as.numeric(hw2_df$character_vector)
```

    ## Warning: NAs introduced by coercion

``` r
as.numeric(hw2_df$factor_vector)
```

Converting the logical variable to numeric changes TRUE to 1 and FALSE
to 0. Converting the character variable to numeric produces NA values
and a warning because its strings cannot be interpreted as numbers.
Converting the factor variable to numeric returns the integer codes of
its levels rather than meaningful numerical values. Therefore, numeric
conversion does not necessarily make a variavle suitable for calculating
a mean.
